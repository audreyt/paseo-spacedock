# Contributing

By contributing material to this repository, you dedicate your copyright in that contribution to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](LICENSE).

Contribute only material whose rights you hold.

Keep personal data out of the project. Do not add names, email addresses, account identifiers, phone numbers, postal addresses, or other personal data to code, docs, examples, fixtures, or sample files.

The license files name no person on purpose. Please leave them that way.
